After four months of intentional quiet, we end our radio silence with a few announcements:

1. [*A Short History of Rocks: or, How to Invent Quantum Computing*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.00005) hit the arxiv in February. It starts with an unconventional history of rocks–from the Pleistocene through Leibniz, Boole and Shannon to the Manhattan project–and ends with an alternate timeline in which John von Neumann discovers quantum computing, 35 years before Feynman! Along the way, we leave a trail of breadcrumbs for those wishing to follow von Neumann's (fictitious) footsteps. Check it out if you like historical science fiction, e.g. *The Difference Engine* or *The Baroque Cycle*.
2. Torsor Labs was selected (along with 38 other startups) for the CDL Quantum 2025 Bootcamp, part of CDL's intake for the 2025 [Quantum stream](https://creativedestructionlab.com/streams/quantum/). The first week has primarily focused on business "basics", though "basic" apparently includes [appropriable quasi-rents](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quasi-rent) and Bayesian entrepreneurship, courtesy of [Kevin Bryan](https://www.kevinbryanecon.com/). Fun stuff!
3. Last but not least, we've been following the trail of breadcrumbs from Leibniz to von Neumann ourselves, and will present the results—an entirely new way of doing quantum computing—some time in the next few weeks. Stay tuned!

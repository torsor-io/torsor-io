1. A [little while back](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.00005), we imagined von Neumann inventing quantum computing in 1947. Since he didn't, we decided to do it instead. [*The Structure and Interpretation of Quantum Programming (SIQP) I: Foundations*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.04527) provides a new framework for quantum computing, uniting C*-algebras with their dual Hilbert spaces to give a rich, expressive, and efficient substrate for programming quantum algorithms and error correction routines. It's a lengthy (~75 page) read, mainly because it is written in a slow, pedagogical style. Check it out!
2. To accompany the release of SIQP I, we've created a [tutorial](https://torsor.io/assets/yaw101.html) on the basics of `yaw`, a language which implements this novel algebraic approach. It's just a preview for now, but explores basic concepts such as algebras, states, and tensor products, and caps off with an application to superdense coding.

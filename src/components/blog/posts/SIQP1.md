A paper drop, a tutorial drop, and some news!

1. A [little while back](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.00005), we imagined von Neumann inventing quantum computing in 1947. Since he didn't, we decided to do it instead. [*The Structure and Interpretation of Quantum Programming (SIQP) I: Foundations*](?) provides a new framework for quantum computing, uniting C*-algebras with their dual Hilbert spaces to give a rich, expressive, and efficient substrate for programming quantum algorithms and error correction routines. It's a lengthy (~70 page) read because it is written pedagogically. Check it out!
2. To accompany the release of SIQP I, we've created a [tutorial](https://torsor.io/assets/yaw101.html) on the basics of `yaw`, a language implementing the algebraic approach. It's embedded in [`Haskell`](https://www.haskell.org/), with ~Python~ interface coming soon. The tutorial introduces basic concepts such as algebras, states, and tensor products, and caps off with an application to superdense coding.
